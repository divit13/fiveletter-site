07 Good questions

Got
questions?

Everything founders usually ask before the first call. Anything else, just write.

osama@fiveletter.in ↗
01 What kind of projects do you take on?

Brand films, campaigns, product stories and social series — from first idea to final cut. If it needs to move someone, it's our kind of brief.

02 How long does a film take?

A single brand film usually runs 4–6 weeks from first call to final cut. Campaigns and series vary — you'll get a clear schedule before we start.

03 What's the investment?

Every project is scoped individually. After the first call you'll get a fixed quote based on scope, shoot days and post — clear pricing, no open-ended scope, no surprises.

04 Do you work outside India?

Yes. We're based in India and work with brands and teams across time zones. Remote-friendly, travel-ready.

05 Who owns the footage and final files?

You do. Final films, cutdowns and agreed assets are yours on delivery. Raw footage can be handed over on request.

06 Can you work with our in-house team?

Absolutely. We can lead end-to-end, plug in as director or producer, or partner with your team on a specific leg of the work.
